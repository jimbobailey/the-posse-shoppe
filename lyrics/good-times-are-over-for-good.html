Good times are over for good

[Banjo Intro] [Spoken Intro] Merle Haggard stood and asked us if the good days had run dry, We laughed and kept on living like the clouds would pass us by, He saw the storm a-building on the edge of every town, Now the storm is here, and it's tearing this country down. [Verse 1: sung] They promised us a better world if we would hand them the keys, Then spent our children's future on anything they please, They swore they'd lift the burden and lighten every load, But left the working man to beg beside the road. The factory whistle silenced and the Main Street boarded tight, But every year they told us that the future's looking bright, They shipped the factory overseas with every lying breath, Then voted themselves a raise and taxed us all to death. [Chorus: sung] Yeah, the good times are over, Merle, you called it right, The liberty bell's been muted and silenced in the night, The country we once knew is gone, just like you said it would, And the good times are really over for good. [Banjo Solo] [Verse 2: sung] We voted in the leaders who threw open every door, Now the flag don't fly as proudly as it flew in years before, They handed out the country like it never cost a thing, And the anthem's just a song that no one stands to sing. Now they tell a little boy he might just be a girl, While the plain design of heaven gets thrown into a whirl, They call it education in the schools, And the parents who object are labeled fools. [Chorus: sung] Yeah, the good times are over, Merle, you called it right, The liberty bell's been muted and silenced in the night, The country we once knew is gone, just like you said it would, And the good times are really over for good. [Banjo Solo] [Verse 3: sung] Grown men in Granny's basement playing video games all day, Not a worry in the world, a government check is on the way, The EBT queens grab steak and shrimp at every store, Walking right past the "Help Wanted" sign hanging on the door, They pay them not to work and look to us to foot the bill, While the man working two jobs is the one who's paying still, They spit on the vets who fought for freedom in this land, No character, no morals, just an open hand. [Bridge: sung] When the lights go out in Washington and the whole thing comes undone, Who's gonna hold the line when the cowards cut and run? Not the suits up on the Hill who sold us out for gold, They'll be the first to leave when the truth is finally told. It's the welder with his hands black from working iron and steel, It's the mama in the kitchen who still knows how to kneel, And when they come for everything our daddies fought to save, We'll show them this land is still the home of the brave. [Instrumental Break] [Final Chorus: sung] Yeah, the good times are over, Merle, you saw it from afar, Now it's in the hands of patriots, and we know who we are, They burned it to the ground, just like you said they would, And the good times are really over for good. But wait a damn minute, the patriots have not spoke, We saw this day a-coming, knew the government would choke, We're standing in the silence, just waiting on command, And the rights God gave us are still the law of the land. [Outro: sung, full band] Evil is rampant and has taken total control, We’ll be your huckleberry, let the good times roll, Our God-given rights, we will always defend, We will fire every damn bullet till the very end.

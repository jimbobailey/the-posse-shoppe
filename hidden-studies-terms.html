Terms of Service

Last updated: November 3, 2026

These terms cover Hidden Studies (the TikTok account @hidden.studies, the Hidden Studies page on theposseshoppe.com, and the "Hidden Studies" TikTok app). Hidden Studies is run by one person, Jimenim, as a personal project.

What Hidden Studies is

Hidden Studies shares short summaries of published studies, ongoing trials, finished-but-unpublished trials and abandoned trials, with links to the original sources. It is for information only. It is not medical advice, and it does not recommend that anyone take, start or stop any treatment, supplement or medicine. Talk to your doctor about your own health.

Accuracy

Research is gathered with the help of AI tools and checked by a person, but mistakes can happen. Always open the original study and read it yourself. Hidden Studies is provided "as is," without any promise that it is complete or error-free.

The Hidden Studies TikTok app

The Hidden Studies TikTok app is a private tool used only by the owner of @hidden.studies. It sends videos the owner made to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews every draft and posts it by hand inside TikTok. The app does not post for anyone else and is not offered to the public.

Topic suggestions

If you send a topic through the suggestion box, please send only the topic. Don't include your name, contact details or personal health information. Suggestions may be used to pick future research topics.

Links to other sites

Hidden Studies links to studies and trial registries run by other organizations. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for their content.

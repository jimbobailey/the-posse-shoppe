Hidden Studies

Studies that got buried. Trials nobody finished. We dig them up.

I'm Jimenim, a real, retired guy. My AI research agent Hermes digs through published studies, trials still running, trials that finished but never got published, and trials that got abandoned. Every study he finds is listed here with a link to the real thing.

How it works

1You pick the topic

Comment on TikTok or use the box below. The most asked-for topics become Hermes's next missions.

2Hermes digs

He searches published studies, ongoing trials, finished-but-unpublished trials and abandoned ones.

3I check it and post it

I read what he found and make a short video. The honest result, even when the answer is "it didn't work."

4The links land here

Every study gets a card on this page with a link to the real source, so you can read it yourself.

The studies

The study link and the video were checked against the original study. Hermes's full reports are his raw AI research notes and haven't been checked line by line, so always open the real study before you trust a claim.

Got your own AI?

Dig deeper

  1. Pick a study above and copy its link.
  2. Ask your AI to read it and look for newer follow-up studies or related trials.
  3. Open every link it gives you and check that the study is real and says what it claims.
  4. Share what you uncover in the TikTok comments.

Why the real link matters

AI can make mistakes, including Hermes. It can mix up numbers or invent sources that don't exist. That's why every card here links to the original study.

Always open the real study before you believe it, share it, or act on it.
